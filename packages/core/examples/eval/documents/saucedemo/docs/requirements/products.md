The cheapest product is the Sauce Labs Onesie and the most expensive is the Sauce Labs Fleece
Jacket.

## Sorting

The list can be sorted four ways, named exactly:

- Name (A to Z)
- Name (Z to A)
- Price (low to high)
- Price (high to low)

When the list first opens it is sorted by Name (A to Z).

## Add and remove buttons

A product not in the cart has a button labelled "Add to cart". Once added, the button is labelled
"Remove" and the cart badge shows the number of products in the cart. With nothing in the cart
the badge is not shown.
