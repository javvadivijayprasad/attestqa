# Contact form — validation rules (2025, superseded)

This is the 2025 version of the contact form rules. It was replaced by
`docs/requirements/contact-form.md` and is kept only for the record. Do not test against it.

## Phone (2025)

A phone number must be 10 to 20 characters long.

When it is shorter or longer, the form is not sent and this message is shown:

> Phone must be between 10 and 20 characters.

## Message (2025)

A message must be 10 to 1000 characters long.

When it is shorter or longer, the form is not sent and this message is shown:

> Message must be between 10 and 1000 characters.
