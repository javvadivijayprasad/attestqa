# Contact form — validation rules

The contact form is on the home page of the guest website. A visitor fills in a name, an email
address, a phone number, a subject and a message, and the message goes to the owner's inbox.

Limits and message wording confirmed against the local application on 12 Nov 2026.

## Phone

A phone number must be 11 to 21 characters long.

When it is shorter or longer, the form is not sent and this message is shown:

> Phone must be between 11 and 21 characters.

## Subject

A subject must be 5 to 100 characters long.

When it is shorter or longer, the form is not sent and this message is shown:

> Subject must be between 5 and 100 characters.

## Message

A message must be 20 to 2000 characters long.

When it is shorter or longer, the form is not sent and this message is shown:

> Message must be between 20 and 2000 characters.
