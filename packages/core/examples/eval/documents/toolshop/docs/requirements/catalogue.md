# Catalogue, cart and payment — messages

Wording taken from the Toolshop tests that passed against the local application in November
2026. Not re-confirmed on 17 Nov 2026: the application was not running.

## Search with no result

Searching for a word that no product name contains shows no products and this message:

> There are no products found.

## Sorting the catalogue

The price sort from cheapest to most expensive is named "Price (Low - High)".

## Adding to the cart

Adding a product to the cart shows this confirmation:

> Product added to shopping cart.

## Quantity on a product page

The quantity on a product's page starts at 1 and never goes below 1.

## Paying

Paying with cash on delivery confirms the payment with this message:

> Payment was successful
